The Expansion Roster

Three mythic commanders join the 10×10 battlefield alongside the classic Staunton army. Each piece is carved from the same solid wood as its army — no resin, no special colouring.

⚔️

The Crusader

C1 · H1 · C10 · H10

Design: A robust medieval great helm, carved in the Staunton tradition — wide cylindrical body with a dome crown, T-shaped visor bars across the front.

Movement: A supreme hybrid heavy-cavalry unit that combines the complete legal movement of a Knight (L-leap) and a Bishop (unlimited diagonal) in a single piece. On any given turn, choose one.

Strategic role: The Crusader's dual mobility makes it one of the most dangerous pieces on the board — it can leap over obstructions like a Knight or sweep long diagonals like a Bishop.

👁

The Spectre

C2 (Blue) · C9 (Blue)

Design: A minimalist shrouded phantom, its flowing robes narrowing to a hooded oval crown — a silhouette that blends naturally with the Staunton set.

Movement: Glides 1 or 2 squares in any direction (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal). Its path must be clear — it cannot jump.

Phase Shift (Invisibility): May be removed from the board for exactly 3 turns. Returning it to any vacant square costs one full move. If not returned after exactly 3 turns, the Spectre is permanently eliminated.

Shadow Infiltration: When capturing, the Spectre lands on the square directly beyond the captured enemy in a straight line. That landing square must be empty.

🔥

The Phoenix

H2 (Orange) · H9 (Orange)

Design: A rising bird in the Staunton style — spread wings, a reaching neck, and an upward beak. Carved entirely in the army's solid wood.

Movement: Moves identically to the King — 1 square in any direction.

Solar Countdown: Survive 15 complete rounds and claim an immediate victory at the start of round 16. Missing the claim forfeits the countdown permanently.

3 Lives (Respawn): When captured, returns to its home square (H2 or H9). Any piece occupying that square is instantly destroyed. On the 3rd capture, the Phoenix is permanently removed.

See The Phoenix tab for the full Fire Tribunal rules.

The Phoenix: Full Mechanics

A high-stakes countdown piece that can win the game on its own — or be permanently silenced at great cost.

Solar Countdown

If the Phoenix survives 15 complete game rounds (15 White moves + 15 Black moves), the owning player may declare an immediate victory at the very start of round 16.

The Forget Rule: If you make any other move instead of claiming victory at the start of round 16, the countdown is irrevocably forfeited for the rest of the game.

Resurrection — 3 Lives

  • On capture 1 or 2: Phoenix returns immediately to its home square (H2 for White / H9 for Black). Solar Countdown resets to 0.
  • Crash Rule: If the home square is occupied at the moment of return, the occupying piece is instantly destroyed — regardless of colour. If it is your own King, you lose immediately.
  • On capture 3: the Phoenix is permanently removed from the game.

The Fire Tribunal — Nuclear Option

When an enemy Phoenix cannot be stopped through conventional means, one extreme countermeasure is available.

Activation Threshold — Immunity in First Life

The Fire Tribunal may only be activated when the enemy Phoenix is on its second or third life. A Phoenix on its first life is completely immune. This guarantees the piece always plays an active opening role and cannot be bought off immediately.

Protocol

  1. At the very start of your turn, loudly announce "Fire Tribunal." This consumes your entire turn — no other piece may move.
  2. Select and remove your own active pieces totalling at least 7 points. Those pieces are permanently gone.
  3. The enemy Phoenix is immediately removed from the board and returned to the box. It never returns. Its Solar Countdown and remaining lives are instantly forfeited.
  4. Your own Phoenix, if present, is completely unaffected.

Strategic Insight

The moment a Phoenix loses its first life, an invisible bounty is placed on its head. From that turn onward, the Phoenix's owner must aggressively eliminate enemy material, reducing the opponent's total available sacrifice value below 7 points — permanently locking out the Fire Tribunal.

Advanced Rules

Three interlocking systems that emerge from the special starting squares.

Blessed Squares

The Spectre and Phoenix starting squares are physically colour-coded on the board. These coloured squares introduce a powerful territorial mechanic that persists even after their pieces move away.

Spectre Squares (Blue)

C2 (White's Spectre home) and C9 (Black's Spectre home) are permanently coloured blue for the entire game.

Phoenix Squares (Orange)

H2 (White's Phoenix home) and H9 (Black's Phoenix home) are permanently coloured orange for the entire game. These are also the Phoenix's respawn squares.

The Vanguard Pawn Upgrade

Any pawn that steps onto an enemy Blessed Square (blue or orange) absorbs its residual energy and is permanently upgraded.

Vanguard Ability

  • From that moment onward, this specific Pawn may move backward AND capture backward, in addition to all standard forward rules.
  • This status is permanent — it stays with the Pawn for the rest of its life.
  • The upgrade applies to the Pawn that physically lands on the square, not to any other Pawn of the same army.

Grand Promotion

When a Pawn reaches the opponent's back rank (row 10 for White / row 1 for Black), it undergoes a Grand Promotion with no limitations on choice.

Unlimited Roster Choice

The promoting player may choose any piece from the full Chessturion roster:

  • Queen, Rook, Bishop, Knight.
  • The Crusader — the Knight-Bishop hybrid.
  • The Spectre — the phantom infiltrator.
  • The Phoenix — begins freshly on its first life, Solar Countdown at 0, granting it full immunity to the Fire Tribunal until it loses that first life.

Classic Rules — Scaled to 10×10

All standard FIDE chess rules remain fully in effect, adapted to the expanded grid.

Rule10×10 Application
Castling The King slides two squares toward a Rook (A- or J-file), after which the Rook jumps to the square the King just crossed. Illegal while in check or if any intervening square is occupied.
En Passant If an enemy pawn advances two squares from its starting row (row 3 or row 8) and lands beside your pawn, you may capture it as though it had moved only one square. Must be executed on your immediate next move.
Pawn Starting Move Pawns may advance one or two squares on their very first move. Row 3 for White, row 8 for Black.
Check & Checkmate Identical to standard chess. The King may not move into check, and any move that leaves the King in check is illegal.
Stalemate If a player to move has no legal moves and their King is not in check, the game ends as a draw. All Phoenix countdowns and extra lives are immediately cancelled.
Promotion See Grand Promotion in the Advanced Rules tab — the full Chessturion roster is available as promotion targets.

Move Priority

When multiple special actions are available on a single turn (e.g. Fire Tribunal announcement, Spectre return, or Vanguard upgrade recognition), the player must resolve one action per turn. The Fire Tribunal announcement and Spectre return are both full-turn actions. The Vanguard upgrade is automatic and instantaneous — no turn cost.
