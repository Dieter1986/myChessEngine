The Phoenix 🔥

Recognisable by its orange, flame-like aesthetic and its solid wooden base. This mighty creature introduces a ticking time-bomb on the board.

Regular Movement

Moves identically to the traditional King: 1 square in any direction.

Special Power 1 — Solar Countdown

If the Phoenix survives 15 complete game rounds (15 moves by White and 15 by Black), the owning player may claim an immediate victory at the start of round 16. The enemy King is declared vanquished.

⚠ THE FORGET RULE: If you fail to claim victory at the very start of round 16 and instead make a normal move, the countdown is irrevocably forfeited for the rest of the game.

Special Power 2 — Resurrection (Respawn)

The Phoenix has exactly 3 lives. When captured, it immediately returns to its starting square (H2 for White / H9 for Black) and the Solar Countdown resets to 0. On the 3rd capture the Phoenix is permanently removed.

💥 THE PHOENIX CRASH RULE: If the respawn square (H2 or H9) is occupied when the Phoenix returns, that piece is instantly destroyed — regardless of colour. If that piece is your own King, you lose the game immediately.

The Shadow 👁

A matte-blue, semi-transparent figure with a smooth abstract face. It excels at guerrilla tactics and deep infiltration.

Regular Movement

Slides 1 or 2 squares in any direction (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal). Its entire path must be clear — it cannot jump over pieces.

Power 1 — Phase Shift (Invisibility)

The Shadow may be removed from the board for exactly 3 turns. Returning it to any free square of your choice costs one full move.

⚠ THE FORGET RULE: If the Shadow is not returned after exactly 3 turns, it is permanently removed from the game.

Power 2 — Shadow Infiltration

The Shadow may capture an enemy piece within range, but must land on the square directly beyond that enemy in a straight line. That landing square must be empty — otherwise the capture is illegal.

The Janus (The Gatekeeper) 🛡

Positioned on the C- and H-files of each back rank. The ultimate tactical connector of the army.

Hybrid Movement

The Janus combines the full attacking power of a Bishop and a Knight. In a single turn it may either glide an unlimited number of squares diagonally, or leap in an L-shape over other pieces. This dual mobility makes it extremely dangerous at breaking open enemy lines.

Classic Rules in Chessturion

All standard chess rules remain in force, scaled to the 100-square grid:

Rule Application on the 10×10 Board
Castling The King slides two squares toward a Rook (A- or J-file), after which the Rook jumps to the square the King just crossed. Not permitted while in check or if any square between them is occupied.
En Passant If an enemy pawn advances two squares from its starting row (row 3 or row 8) and lands beside your pawn, you may capture it as if it had moved only one square. This capture must be made on your very next move.
Promotion A pawn reaching the far end of the board (row 10 for White, row 1 for Black) is immediately promoted to any officer of the owning player's choice: Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.
Stalemate If a player to move has no legal moves and their King is not in check, the game ends immediately as a draw. All countdowns and extra lives are cancelled at that moment.
